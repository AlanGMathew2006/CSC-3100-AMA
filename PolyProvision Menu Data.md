# Campus restaurant menu data

Items, prices, calories, protein, carbs and fat for the four chain restaurants on the Cal Poly SLO campus. Each row says where its price and nutrition came from and whether the item is confirmed on the campus menu. Sort any column or use protein per dollar to compare value.

[ ] Priced only

## Combos and meals

Panda bases and entrees are priced through combos: a meal is one base row plus N entree rows. Premium entrees add $1.50 per slot. Taco Bell combos include a drink, and Chick-fil-A breakfast meals add a side and drink.

## Gaps to close

- **A few Chick-fil-A sizes** (12 ct nuggets, large fries and mac & cheese) aren't priced separately on Grubhub.
- **Taco Bell items missing from Grubhub** (quesadillas, Nachos BellGrande, Cheesy Roll Up, Cinnamon Twists, Spicy Potato Soft Taco) have no price yet.
- **In-person only options** like custom Panda combos and the Bigger Plate use the same base pricing; the Bigger Plate price comes from the nearest SLO Panda.
- **13 items** have calories or a price but no published protein, carbs or fat.
